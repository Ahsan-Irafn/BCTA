Qualification Description
This qualification is designed to meet the needs of builders, including selecting contractors, overseeing the work and its quality, and liaising with clients.
Entry &amp; Admission Requirements
Applicants must be aged 18 or above
To assist prospective students to determine if the course is suitable and appropriate for their individual needs, Pretraining Review (PTR) will be conducted.

This course is delivered with a weekly class (currently is virtually online on MS Teams). You need to set aside approximately 10 hours per week for self-study and 5 hours per week for assessment activities.
In weekly class, your trainer will facilitate a range of Training experiences including:
&#8211; Trainer led instruction
&#8211; Discussion and debate
&#8211; Group Training activities
&#8211; Case studies and Role plays

To be eligible for Skills SA subsidised training, you need to meet these requirements:
Citizenship and Residency
You must be:
an Australian or New Zealand citizen, or
an Australian permanent resident, or
an eligible visa holder.
Location
You must live or work in South Australia.
Age
You must be 16 years or over and not enrolled at school. BCTA enrols students aged 18 and over.
Course Limits
You may undertake up to two subsidised courses at the same time.
Upfront Assessment of Need
All Skills SA funded students complete an Upfront Assessment of Need (UAN) before enrolment.

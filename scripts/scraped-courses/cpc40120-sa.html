Qualification Description

This qualification is designed to meet the needs of builders and managers of small to medium-sized building businesses.

Entry & Admission Requirements
  • Applicants must be aged 18 or above
  • To assist prospective students to determine if the course is suitable and appropriate for their individual needs, Pretraining Review (PTR) will be conducted.
  • Successful completion of BCTA’s Language, Literacy, Numeracy and Digital (LLND)

This course is delivered with a weekly class (conducted virtually in a real-time classroom environment through MS Teams). You need to set aside 10 hours per week for self-study and 5 hours for Assessment work. In weekly class, your trainer will facilitate a range of Training experiences including:

  • Trainer led instruction
  • Discussion and debate
  • Group Training activities
  • Case studies
  • Role plays
Government Funded

Amount to be paid by the student = $446 (Student Contribution Fee)

This training is delivered with South Australian Government funding through Skills SA for eligible students.

*Check if you are eligible for subsidised training.
https://mytraining.skills.sa.gov.au/training/getting-started/check-eligibility

To be eligible for Skills SA subsidised training, you need to meet these requirements:

Citizenship and residency
You must be:

  1. an Australian or New Zealand citizen, or
  2. an Australian permanent resident, or
  3. an eligible visa holder.

Location
You must live or work in South Australia.

Age
You must be 16 years or over and not enrolled at school. BCTA enrols students aged 18 and over.

Course limits
You may undertake up to two subsidised courses at the same time.

Upfront Assessment of Need
All Skills SA funded students complete an Upfront Assessment of Need (UAN) before enrolment.
